# ✈️ AERONYX
### AI-Powered Predictive Maintenance & Self-Healing Fleet Intelligence

> **Predict the Failure. Preserve the Fleet.**

AERONYX is an intelligent aircraft predictive maintenance and fleet readiness platform designed to go beyond traditional failure prediction.

It combines **Collective Fleet Intelligence, Digital Twins, Failure Domino Simulation, and Self-Healing Fleet Recovery** to anticipate potential aircraft failures, explore their fleet-wide impact, and recommend intelligent recovery strategies.

---

## 🔗 About

Welcome to **AERONYX — The Self-Healing Fleet Intelligence System**.

Traditional predictive maintenance systems focus on identifying which aircraft component might fail next.

**AERONYX goes one step further.**

It explores how a single aircraft failure could affect the entire fleet, including maintenance schedules, spare-part availability, technician workload, and operational readiness.

Developed as an interactive prototype for **Smart India Hackathon 2026 (SIH)**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🧠 Collective Fleet Intelligence
- Identifies recurring failure patterns across aircraft.
- Explores shared maintenance insights to detect risks earlier.
- Introduces **Maintenance DNA**, enabling fleet-wide learning.

### ⚡ Failure Domino Simulator
- Simulates how one aircraft failure affects the entire fleet.
- Visualizes impacts on spare parts, technicians, and maintenance schedules.
- Supports interactive what-if scenarios.

### 🔧 Self-Healing Fleet Engine
- Generates simulated recovery recommendations.
- Suggests maintenance priorities and resource allocation.
- Supports intelligent fleet recovery planning.

### 🛩️ Aircraft Digital Twin
- Provides interactive aircraft health visualization.
- Supports component-level monitoring and fault simulation.
- Helps users understand aircraft conditions.

### 📊 Fleet Readiness Dashboard
- Displays fleet health and aircraft availability.
- Visualizes maintenance risks and operational disruptions.
- Explores projected readiness over 7–30 days.

### 🔍 Explainable Intelligence
- Explains simulated risk assessments.
- Provides understandable recovery recommendations.
- Keeps operational decisions under human control.

## ⚙️ How It Works

```text
Aircraft Data
     |
     v
Failure Risk Assessment
     |
     v
Fleet Impact Simulation
     |
     v
Intelligent Recovery Planning
     |
     v
Fleet Readiness Dashboard
```

1. **Monitor** — Explore aircraft health and fleet status.
2. **Predict** — Identify potential maintenance risks.
3. **Simulate** — Examine fleet-wide failure consequences.
4. **Recover** — Generate recommended recovery actions.
5. **Evaluate** — Compare fleet readiness before and after recovery.

---

## 🎯 What Makes AERONYX Different?

Most predictive maintenance systems ask:

> Which aircraft component is likely to fail next?

**AERONYX asks:**

> If one aircraft fails tomorrow, how can we prevent that failure from disrupting the entire fleet?

Our innovation connects **failure prediction, fleet-wide impact analysis, and intelligent recovery planning** into one unified platform.
